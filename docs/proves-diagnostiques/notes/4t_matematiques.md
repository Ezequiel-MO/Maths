## 1. What did the maths part of the 4t (and earlier 3r) diagnostic tests look like? Concrete examples.

### Takeaway
The 4t test (LOMLOE-era; first applied in April 2024) has a fixed "blueprint" of 20 maths questions per edition (2023-24 has 24 scored items because some questions have sub-parts). The blueprint is the same each year: 5 senses (numeric 5, measure 4, spatial 4, algebraic 2, stochastic 5), 4 competence blocks, and 3 cognitive levels weighted 30/45/25. Every edition wraps the maths questions in a single story context (a school trip, birdwatching, waste and compost). The items ask for calculation with whole numbers and a little decimals, money-free measurement, calendars, reading and building bar graphs and tables, routes on maps and grids, solids and plane figures, symmetry, patterns and simple probability. In 2023-24 and 2024-25 about half the items were open ("justifica la resposta", with partial credit); in 2025-26 all 20 maths items are multiple choice.

**D. Real example items, 2023-24 (context "Les colònies a l'observatori Travis"; all in the official booklet)**
- Item 1 (MC): calendar of June 2024; camp is "d'un dilluns a un dimecres" in the first fortnight; pick the dates (3-6, 10-12, 10-13, 17-20 June). Measure/time, pattern of week.
- Item 2 (open short): coach arrives 30 minutes before a 9:00 departure; what time? (time subtraction.)
- Item 3 (MC): 23 pupils in 4t A, 24 in 4t B, 3 teachers per class; which operation gives the seats needed? Options 23+24+3; 23+24+3x2; 23x24+3x2; 23x24x3x2. (Correct is 23+24+3x2 = 53, my calculation.)

- Item 6 (open): complete a table from a double bar graph (workshops Sol, Lluna, Estrelles, Planetes; classes A and B; max 8 pupils per class per workshop).
- Item 7.1 to 7.4 (Sí/No, open coding): which questions can be answered with the graph data? (most chosen workshop, "chosen before", etc.; tests what a graph can and cannot say.)
- Item 8 (MC): which of four graphs (pictogram with key 1 vote / 5 votes, bar graphs, tally) matches a data table.
- Item 9 (open, superior): an email with a table of "second choice" results has an error; circle it and explain how you found it. (Total of 22 listed does not match the class size 4+5+7+8 = 24. My calculation.)

- Item 12 (open): draw a square on a grid using a given blue line; item 13 (open, tick Sí/No and justify by drawing): can 4 triangular tables be grouped in a given shape as 3 were.

**G. Recurring contexts and question styles**
- One storyline per edition frames every area: camp at an observatory (2023-24), birds (2024-25), waste/compost (2025-26). Maths questions are embedded in the story rather than stand alone. VERIFIED — the three booklets.
- Recurring skill types, in all three editions: reading and building bar graphs, pictograms and tables (always 4 or 5 categories; axes in steps of 100 or 200); calendars (with "primera quinzena", "primer dimecres", "2 setmanes i 3 dies") and clock time; maps/grids/plans with letter-number coordinates and shortest route; unit conversion and measuring with a ruler; whole-number multiplication, division and doubling in word problems; solids and faces; symmetry; repeating patterns ("sèrie"); simple probability (certain/possible/impossible, more/less likely); "which student is right" error-detection and "write a problem" tasks. VERIFIED across the booklets above.
- Money does not appear in the 4t maths items I read (it does in 3r 2016-17 and 2015-16, see below). INFERRED from reading the three 4t editions.
- Decimals: appear as kilograms (3.6 kg, 14.5 kg, 4.3 kg), metres (1.72 m) and centimetres (2.5 cm), added, ordered or read; no fraction notation beyond "mig quilogram"/"meitat" in the 4t items I read. VERIFIED from items above.
- Calculator: none of the booklets, guides, or the application instructions I read mention a calculator; the instruction line says pupils may write calculations in the booklet. Treat as "not stated, no evidence it is allowed". VERIFIED absence in the documents read.

### Inferences
- Because the blueprint (5/4/4/2/5 senses; 8/6/3/3 blocks; 6/9/5 grades) repeated exactly in three editions, the 2026-27 test (26 to 28 April 2027) will probably keep it, but the 2026-27 documents do not exist yet.
- With the shift to machine-corrected answer sheets in 2025-26, open "justifica" items disappeared from the maths part; the 2027 edition may stay closed-format. The earlier editions needed teacher coding. INFERRED from the 2025-26 specification (only MC item types listed).
- A training game that covers: multi-step whole-number word problems with decimals in kg/m/cm, bar graphs and tables (read, compare, convert to graph), calendars and time, grid/map routes, solids and faces, symmetry, patterns and simple probability would span the sampled items.

### Gaps
- No page or PDF gives the time for the maths part alone. For 2025-26 each booklet takes 2 hours (plus 15 minutes of non-assessed introduction on the first day) and mixes 2 or 3 areas (see section 4). Times for 2023-24 and 2024-25 not found.
- Score weight of "resposta parcial" versus "resposta completa" is not stated in any public document: the guides give per-item weights (each of 20 items is 0.5 point) but the "Codificació de respostes i criteris de correcció" document for open items is distributed to schools through the AVI application and I found no public copy.
- 2025-26 answer keys are images in the Marc PDF (page 13 to 16); my text extraction could not read them.
- No official sample test ("prova model") for 4t was found; only the released real tests from the three editions exist on the APE site.

## 2. Is any other area (science, medi) tested in 4t? Format and examples.

### Takeaway
Yes: "Coneixement del Medi Natural, Social i Cultural" is tested in every edition as a separate 20-item area, interleaved in the same themed booklets. In 2023-24 it was a separate booklet with an astronomy theme (including justification questions and graph reading); in 2024-25 and 2025-26 its items are mixed into the same notebooks as maths. There is no separate "science" test; science is part of medi (the blocs "Cultura científica", "Societats i territoris", "Tecnologia i digitalització").

### Cited Findings
- 2025-26: 20 items across Q1 (items 15, 16, 17), Q2 (9, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17), Q4 (16 to 20), Q5 (12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20). ## 5. The earlier 3r de primària diagnostic (historical)

### Takeaway
Before the LOMLOE 4t test, Catalonia ran a competency diagnostic at 3r de primària (elaborated externally by the Departament / CSASE, applied and corrected internally by schools, reported to schools only). The maths part used four dimensions and mixed multiple choice, short answers and "explica com ho has fet" items scored 0, 1 or 2. The one full corrected paper I found is course 2016-17. I did not find how and when the 3r test ended.

### Cited Findings
- Description from a school slide deck on the 2015-16 edition: the test is centred on basic competences, assesses linguistic and mathematical competence, "L'elaboració de les proves són externes, però l'aplicació, la correcció i l'anàlisi dels resultats són internes", with orientation purpose for schools. Maths dimensions: Numeració i càlcul; Espai, forma i mesura; Relacions i canvi; Estadística i atzar. Example items shown: choose the best unit (km, m, cm) for the width of a supermarket bag, a pencil's length, distance between villages; circle the banknotes that make 40 euros. SECONDARY (school presentation of an official test) — [AVALUACIÓ DIAGNÒSTICA TERCER DE PRIMÀRIA 2015-2016](https://blocs.xtec.cat/acordcoresponsabilitat/files/2016/07/AVALUACI%C3%93-DIAGN%C3%92STICA-3r-curs-15-16-A.pdf)
- A school blog (2 February 2018) states the 3r test is prepared by the Consell Superior d'Avaluació del Sistema Educatiu, assesses communicative linguistic and mathematical competence midway through 3r, and was run in the first two weeks of March 2018. SECONDARY — [CEIP Tarra](https://agora.xtec.cat/ceiptarra/general/proves-diagnostiques-de-3r/) and its [category page](https://agora.xtec.cat/ceiptarra/categoria/primaria/cicle-mitja/page/32/).
- Corrected maths test, "curs 2016-2017" (11 pages, posted by a school in Feb 2018): five activities, two sessions. Session 1 activities 1 to 3 (ruler not allowed); session 2 activities 4 and 5, where the teacher reads question 4.1 aloud twice. Instructions: some questions mark an X, others write the answer, "Has d'escriure les operacions que facis". Each item shows its score range (0-1 or 0-1-2) and an "Idea bàsica" model answer. Theme: pupils prepare a nature exhibition. VERIFIED (as a document) but hosted by a school — [CORRECCIÓ-Prova-matemàtiques.pdf](https://blocs.xtec.cat/eltarra/files/2018/02/CORRECCI%C3%93-Prova-matem%C3%A0tiques.pdf)
 - Activity 1 "El taller de pintura": name the 2 groups with most pupils (from a picture), complete a pattern of cardboards, "11 pinzells, 2 per pot, 5 pots: sobrarà o faltarà algun? explica com ho saps" (0-1-2), draw "el doble de llapis" than 3 erasers, which box matches a top view, cut a card into 4 equal squares, circle coins making 65 cents.
 - Activity 2 "El gran mural": complete a bar graph from a table of votes (blau 10, lila 2, groc 8, verd 6), total pupils (MC), "segon color més votat, explica" (0-1-2).
