# State-level and international assessments of 4º de primaria that Catalan pupils may sit (besides Catalonia's own avaluació diagnòstica)

Research date: 2026-10-18. Pupil in question: 4º primaria in school year 2026-27 (so born about 2017; was in 3º in 2025-26).
Labels: [OFFICIAL] = Ministerio/INEE, IEA/Boston College, BOE or Generalitat (APE) page that I fetched and read. [PRESS/SNIPPET] = press or a search-result snippet I did not open in full. [INFERRED] = my reasoning.

## 1. State evaluation (Ministerio de Educación) of 4º de primaria: did it run, is one planned for 2026-27, what format?

### Takeaway
There is NO state "Evaluación General del Sistema" (EGS) in 4º de primaria. The LOMLOE EGS is defined for 6º de primaria and 4º de ESO, sample-based and multi-year. The Ministry's only 4º-primaria test is the *Evaluación de diagnóstico* (census, annual), but the INEE runs it only in Ministry-managed territory (Ceuta, Melilla, CIDEAD, schools abroad), so Catalan pupils do not sit the Ministry's version. Catalonia runs its own census diagnostic in 4t (covered by another researcher). The brief's "LOMLOE art. 144" is the legal basis of the diagnostic evaluations and the common framework; the EGS itself is art. 143 LOE.

### Cited Findings

#### The EGS (art. 143 LOE as modified by LOMLOE): grades, sampling, dates
- The EGS is regulated in art. 143 LOE (modified by LOMLOE), is applied in **6.º de Educación Primaria and 4.º de ESO**, with **carácter muestral y plurianual**; data are representative per comunidad autónoma and for Spain; it covers competences (not individual marks); informative/formative/orientadora for schools, informative for families. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE, Evaluación general del sistema educativo](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-nacionales/evaluacion-general-sistema.html)
- Competences planned in the first edition: Comunicación lingüística, STEM, Digital, Plurilingüe. First edition for 6.º EP was planned for 2025-26, "formato completamente digital". Pilots: 6.º EP May-June 2023 (linguistic and STEM) and May-June 2025 (plurilingual and digital). [OFFICIAL] — same INEE page.
- Main study 2026 (6.º EP): fieldwork April-May 2026, digital; 50 randomly chosen schools per comunidad autónoma plus all schools in Ceuta and Melilla; in schools with 51+ pupils a sample of 40 pupils, otherwise all pupils; run in the five co-official languages; digital context questionnaires for pupils, school leadership, teachers and specialist teachers, and a paper questionnaire for families. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE, Primera edición EGS 6.º EP 2025-2026](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-nacionales/evaluacion-general-sistema/primera-edicion-egs-6ep-2025-2026.html)
- For 2026-27 the Ministry's only listed EGS activity is a **pilot in 4.º ESO**: window 12 April to 11 May 2027, digital, STEM + comunicación lingüística + plurilingüe + digital, with a context questionnaire, 50 schools (30 from comunidades with only Castilian: Canarias, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha, Extremadura, La Rioja; 5 each from comunidades with another co-official language, including Cataluña). Nothing for 4º primaria. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE, Prueba piloto EGS 4.º ESO 2026-2027](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-nacionales/evaluacion-general-sistema/prueba-piloto-egs-4eso-2026-2027.html)
- Catalan press guide (3Cat, published 4 May 2026, updated 10 June 2026) says the Ministry's avaluació general runs every 3 years in 6è primària (4 to 29 May 2026 in Catalonia; plurilingual, language, STEM and digital competences; does not count in pupil records; 69 schools selected) and in 4t ESO "from 2027-28". The same article says Catalonia's own "proves de final d'etapa" (6è primària and 4t ESO) are now sample-based and not held in years when the Ministry test runs. [PRESS; the 69-school figure is attributed to 6è primària by the article, but another search summary attributed it to the 4t ESO tests, so treat it as unverified] — [3Cat guide](https://www.3cat.cat/3catinfo/comenca-una-nova-prova-a-les-escoles-guia-de-tots-els-examens-per-avaluar-resultats-educatius/noticia/3405553/)

## 2. PIRLS 2026 (reading comprehension, 4th grade): Catalonia, format, released items

### Takeaway
PIRLS 2026 already took place in the previous school year (Catalonia: 7 April to 29 May 2026, 50 sampled schools, with an extended Catalan sample), so the child's cohort (in 3º then) did not take it and there is no PIRLS in 2026-27; results are due December 2027. The test is fully digital, group-adaptive (not per-pupil adaptive), about 2 x 40 minutes plus a 30-minute student questionnaire. Next PIRLS cycle is 2031 (5-year cycle; inferred).

### Cited Findings
- Catalonia 2026: Catalan pupils of 4º primaria in a sample of **50 schools** took part, organised with support of the APE and the INEE; the school sample was drawn by the INEE with randomness and representativeness criteria; window 7 April to 29 May 2026; digital on the INEE platform; about 90 minutes in two sessions of about 40 minutes with a short break; context questionnaires for pupils, teachers, school leadership and families; Catalonia takes part with an extended sample ("mostra ampliada"). [OFFICIAL, Generalitat/APE] — [APE, PIRLS](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/ca/avaluacions/internacionals/pirls) (page updated 08.06.2026)
- Schools were notified on 16 Feb 2026; it is the third time Catalonia participates with an extended sample; the test "ocuparà un matí"; more than 60 countries; IEA takes 15-18 months to process and publish results. [OFFICIAL] — [APE news "Com serà PIRLS 2026" (16/02/2026)](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/ca/actualitat/detall/2026/02/com-sera-pirls-2026)
- APE news of 13 May 2026 ("Vols saber com és la prova...") said the test was being taken "these days" and pointed to PIRLS 2021 released items in Catalan. [OFFICIAL] — [APE, 13/05/2026](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/ca/actualitat/detall/2026/05/vols-saber-com-es-pirls)
- Spain: main study 13 April to 29 May 2026 in more than 500 schools; pilot May 2025 in 30 schools of Asturias, Aragón, La Rioja, Castilla-La Mancha, Madrid (Catalonia not in the pilot). New in 2026: fully digital, new interface to scroll through texts and select/answer questions, ePIRLS tasks integrated, home/school context data, process data. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE, PIRLS 2026](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-internacionales/pirls/pirls-2026.html)
- IEA: 64 education systems participate; all fully digital; Encyclopedia November 2027, results December 2027, public database February 2028. [OFFICIAL] — [IEA PIRLS 2026](https://www.iea.nl/studies/iea/pirls/2026)
- Assessment design (Frameworks, chapter on design): 18 text blocks, half literary experience and half "acquire and use information"; one-third easy, one-third medium, one-third difficult; each pupil gets two blocks (one literary, one informational) in two parts; **group-adaptive** = the proportion of easier/harder booklets is tailored to each country's population, not to each pupil; online informational tasks (ePIRLS) are included in the "acquire and use information" purpose; administration = two 40-minute sessions plus a 30-minute student questionnaire. [OFFICIAL] — [PIRLS 2026 Assessment Frameworks PDF](https://isc.bc.edu/pirls2026/frameworks/downloads/P26_Assessment-Frameworks.pdf)
- Texts: passages typically 500-800 words; online tasks about three websites, 5 to 10 web pages, about 1000 words in total; four comprehension processes (retrieve explicit information, straightforward inferences, interpret and integrate ideas, evaluate and critique); response formats include multiple choice, written response and, new in digital, drag-and-drop and matching. [OFFICIAL] — [PIRLS 2026 Reading Assessment Framework ch.1](https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/pirls2026/frameworks/downloads/P26_Ch1_Reading-Assessment.pdf)
- Spain's INEE leaflet says PIRLS is run every 5 years and Spain participates since 2006; student, teacher, school-leader and family questionnaires. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE poster PIRLS 2026](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/dam/jcr:8c4b1265-87bd-4fa0-b9d7-6e8926aa80aa/cartel-pirls-2026.pdf)
- Previous result, PIRLS 2021: Catalonia 507 points vs Spain 521; down 15 from 2016 (3Cat). [PRESS] — [3Cat](https://www.3cat.cat/3catinfo/catalunya-a-la-cua-en-comprensio-lectora-a-4t-de-primaria-perd-15-punts-en-5-anys/noticia/3232065/)

## 3. TIMSS (2023 results, 2027 cycle): Catalan participation, 4th grade format, released items

### Takeaway
TIMSS (maths and science, 4th grade) ran in Spain in May 2023 and Catalonia took part with an extended sample (980 pupils, 49 schools) and scored 489 in maths (Spain 498). The next cycle, **TIMSS 2027, has its main study in spring (April-May) 2027, i.e. during the child's 4º primaria year (2026-27)**, with about 600 Spanish schools drawn at random and 12 comunidades with an extended sample. Whether Catalonia is in the 2027 extended sample is not confirmed in what I found (it participated with its own extended sample in 2015, 2019 and 2023 per the APE page). It is digital, group-adaptive, with individual routing to very-easy blocks for very-low scorers, 2 x 36 minutes plus a 30-minute questionnaire.

### Cited Findings
- TIMSS 2023: 59 countries and 6 benchmarking entities at 4th grade. In Spain the main study took place in May 2023 in more than 500 schools with about 10 000 pupils; 9 comunidades extended their sample: Andalucía, Canarias, Castilla y León, **Cataluña**, Madrid, Navarra, Galicia, Illes Balears and Asturias. International and Spanish reports published 4 Dec 2024. [OFFICIAL] — [INEE TIMSS 2023](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-internacionales/timss/timss-2023.html)
- Catalonia 2023: 980 pupils of 4t primària from 49 schools (33 public, 16 private), schools chosen at random proportional to size, one class per school; maths 489 vs Spain 498 vs OECD 525 (Catalan data summarised by search result from Consell Superior d'Avaluació synthesis, Quaderns d'avaluació 66). [PRESS/SNIPPET for the 980/49/33/16 and 489 figures; the Catalan repository page was blocked when I tried to read it] — [Repositori Generalitat, TIMSS 2023 síntesi](https://repositori.educacio.gencat.cat/handle/20.500.12694/7395); [Diari de l'Educació](https://diarieducacio.cat/?p=96381)
- APE: "Catalunya hi participa des de l'any 2015 amb una mostra pròpia ampliada"; tests apply to a representative sample of schools every four years; the TIMSS 2027 section only links to IEA and INEE pages (no Catalan sample size given). [OFFICIAL] — [APE, TIMSS](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/ca/avaluacions/internacionals/timss)
- TIMSS 2027 in Spain: ninth cycle; digital; pilot 7-27 April 2026 in 30 schools of Andalucía, Canarias, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha and Castilla y León (Catalonia not in the pilot); instruments being prepared; **main study spring 2027**; features: curriculum-based frameworks, innovative digital tasks, "evaluación adaptativa por grupo". [OFFICIAL] — [INEE TIMSS 2027](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/inee/evaluaciones-internacionales/timss/timss-2027.html)
- INEE poster TIMSS 2027: main study April-May 2027; about 600 schools (public, private and concertados) randomly selected; 12 comunidades with extended sample will have own results; target pupils 4º de Educación Primaria; maths (number, measurement and geometry, data) and science (biology, physical science, earth science); context questionnaires for students, teachers, principals and families; national and international results December 2028. [OFFICIAL] — [Cartel TIMSS 2027](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/dam/jcr:7a0cd5f0-d1ea-41b6-b10b-8407a596e49c/cartel-timss-2027-accesible.pdf)
- Design, from the 2027 frameworks: group-adaptive design retained from 2023 with the 14-block structure per subject and grade (five easy, four medium, five difficult blocks; 8 trend blocks from 2023 + 6 new); new **individual-level routing**: a pupil who scores fewer than 3 points on the automatically scored items of Part 1 gets one very-easy maths block and one very-easy science block in Part 2; fourth grade = two 36-minute sessions (Part 1, Part 2) then a 30-minute student questionnaire; digital item types include selection, drag-and-drop, number-pad and open response, plus (2027 brochure) free drawing and on-screen constructions of graphs/diagrams; automated and machine-assisted scoring. [OFFICIAL] — [TIMSS 2027 Assessment Frameworks](https://dok.rak.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/TIMSS-2027-Frameworks.pdf) (copy hosted on a third-party site; the IEA announcement is [here](https://www.iea.nl/node/4753)); [TIMSS 2027 brochure](https://elvis.bc.edu/timss2027/TIMSS2027_Brochure.pdf)
- Example tasks named in the 2027 brochure: a maths item "determine the cost and the amount of drinks to buy as part of planning a school party"; a science task on measuring animal footprints to find which animal eats garden plants. [OFFICIAL] — [TIMSS 2027 brochure](https://elvis.bc.edu/timss2027/TIMSS2027_Brochure.pdf)
- TIMSS 2023 grade 4 maths: fully digital; 187 items in 14 blocks; content split number 50%, measurement and geometry 30%, data 20%; includes Problem Solving and Inquiry Tasks.
