# Avaluació de diagnòstic de 4t de primària (Catalunya): parts de llengües (català, castellà, anglès/francès) i antecedent de 3r

Method note: "VERIFIED" = read by me in the official PDF/page from avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat (Agència d'Avaluació i Prospectiva de l'Educació, APE; before 2025 the Consell Superior d'Avaluació del Sistema Educatiu, CSASE). "SECONDARY" = school/news page. "INFERRED" = my deduction. Booklet text was extracted from PDF; images (picture options, drawings) were not seen.

## 1. What did the language parts of the 4t (and earlier 3r) diagnostic tests look like? (concrete texts and questions)

### Takeaway
Since 2023-24 (first LOMLOE edition) the 4t test has had only receptive skills in the language areas: oral comprehension (audio, played twice) and reading comprehension, 23 items per language area (Catalan, Spanish) and 20 to 32 items in English/French, all on one shared theme per year. There is no extended writing task, no oral expression and no read-aloud fluency task in 4t. 2023-24 and 2024-25 mixed multiple choice with a few short open answers; 2025-26 is multiple choice/true-false/matching only (machine-scored). The 3r predecessor (2017-18) did have separate oral comprehension and reading, written expression and oral expression tests in Catalan, but I could not retrieve its content.

### Cited Findings

**Framework and format (all years)**
- Language tests (Catalan, Spanish) each have: one oral text + one narrative (literary) text + one informative text. Item counts: 9 oral + 6 narrative + 8 informative = 23 items (2023-24, 2024-25 verified for Catalan and Spanish; 2025-26 Catalan 9+6+8 = 23) — [AD4P-26 Marc, Especificacions i Claus](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2025-2026/AD4P-26_Marc_Especificacions_Claus_de_respostes.pdf); [Guia d'anàlisi 2024-25](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2024-2025/quaderns-proves/guia-analisi.pdf); [Guia d'anàlisi 2023-24](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2023-2024/quaderns-proves/guia-analisi.pdf)
- Items are built from the INEE "Marco general de las evaluaciones del sistema educativo" and from the "indicadors d'assoliment" derived from the evaluation criteria of the specific competences — [2025-26 Marc](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2025-2026/AD4P-26_Marc_Especificacions_Claus_de_respostes.pdf)
- Each year's tests (all areas, including maths and medi) share one theme: 2023-24 "El camí de les estrelles"; 2024-25 "Descobrim els ocells" (guide); 2025-26 waste/recycling (booklet titles "Una mirada als residus", "Detectius de residus", "El poder dels residus", "Nova vida als residus") — guides above + [2025-26 released page](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/ca/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/proves-corregides/2025-2026)
- Language items are interleaved in shared booklets with maths and medi items (2025-26: Catalan items are spread over Quadern 1 (items 1-9 oral, 18-23 narrative) and Quadern 2 (items 1-8 informative); Spanish over Quadern 4 and 5) — 2025-26 Marc + booklets. English, French and Aranese have their own separate booklet.

**Item-type vocabulary used officially**
- "Opció múltiple simple" (4 options in Catalan/Spanish; 3 in foreign languages 2024-25), "opció múltiple complexa" = dicotòmica (Sí/No, Vertader/Fals, True/False) or "de relació/ordenació" (match two columns, order items), and (2023-24, 2024-25 only) "resposta oberta (curta o construïda)" — Guia 2024-25 p.9; Marc 2025-26 only lists simple MC and complex MC (dichotomous, relation), i.e. open items were dropped.

**Response/correction mechanics**
- 2023-24 and 2024-25: administered and corrected by the school's teachers (coding responses, typing into the AVI application); 2023-24 window 2-30 April 2024 (Resolució EDU/350/2024); 2024-25 window 24 April-16 May 2025 — Guies 2023-24 and 2024-25
- Open answers are coded in categories: "resposta errònia / parcial / completa" (for some "errònia / completa"); the detailed criteria are in a separate school-only document "Codificació de respostes i criteris de correcció" per area (referenced in the guide; not on the public page) — Guia 2024-25 §4.1.7; [Solucionari 2024-25](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2024-2025/solucionari-ad4p-24-25.pdf); [Solucions 2023-24](https://avaluacioeducativa.gencat.cat/content/dam/avaluacioeducativa/avaluacio-educativa/avaluacions/diagnostic/quart-primaria/2023-2024/solucions-items.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Because 2025-26 items are all closed and scanned, the Department moved to a fully machine-scored model; if a new game mirrors the test it should use 4-option MC, true/false, ordering 4 statements, vocabulary-in-context, "what is the text for / what is the best title / what does the story teach" items, plus a reading-type item ("is it a tale, news item, poem, description?").
- INFERRED: Texts in the language tests are short (roughly 300-450 words each; the narrative fits ~1 page and the informative ~2 pages in the booklets) and tied to a single everyday-science theme; each year ends with a "tema" that also appears in maths/medi items. Word counts were not measured.
- INFERRED: A reading-aloud/fluency measure and extended writing are absent in 4t; the written/oral expression assessments in Catalonia appear in the 6è (and 4t ESO) competències bàsiques instead (search-result claim, not verified from an official page).

## 2. What competence dimensions are assessed per the official framework?

### Takeaway
Two "destreses" per language area (comprensió oral, comprensió escrita) crossed with four "processos cognitius" (localitzar i obtenir informació; realitzar inferències directes; integrar i interpretar idees i informacions; reflexionar i valorar continguts i informacions). Each item has an "indicador d'assoliment". Scores are 0-10 with four achievement levels.

### Cited Findings
- Destreses: "Comprensió oral" and "Comprensió escrita" (capacity to understand, interpret and value oral/written, literary and informative texts); situations: literary vs informative — Guia 2024-25 §4.1.2-4.1.4; Guia 2023-24
- Four cognitive processes, with sample indicators ("Localitza informació concreta del text", "Infereix informació del text", "Interpreta informació del text", "Dedueix el significat de paraules segons el context", "Dedueix l'ordre de les parts d'un text", "Reflexiona sobre la finalitat/tipologia del text", "Localitza la idea principal del text") — Guia 2024-25 §4.1.5, §4.4.5
- Weights (2023-24 and 2024-25, Catalan and Spanish): oral comprehension 40% (4 points: locate 6%, infer 14%, integrate 14%, reflect 6%); written comprehension 60% (6 points: 9/21/21/9 %) — Guies 2023-24, 2024-25. English: oral 50% / written 50% (locate 20%, infer 15%, integrate 12%, reflect 3% in each) — Guia 2023-24 §4.4.6.
- Achievement levels by score: NA 0-<4.95; satisfactori 4.95-<6.95; notable 6.95-<8.95; excel·lent 8.95-10 (in 2024-25 abbreviated NA/AS/AN/AE) — Guies.
- Process distribution 2025-26 (Catalan oral: locate 2 items, infer 3, integrate 2, reflect 2; English oral: 3/3/2/2; English reading 2/3/3/2; Spanish oral 2/3/2/2) — Marc 2025-26.
- Maths and medi use different dimensions (blocs competencials/sentits; eixos fonamentals) — Marc 2025-26 (out of scope).
- The 2025-26 families sheet says the test assesses "les competències específiques" of each area (Matemàtiques, Llengua Catalana i Literatura, Llengua Castellana i Literatura, Llengua Estrangera (anglesa, francesa), Coneixement del Medi) — [Families sheet](https://agora.xtec.cat/ceipgermanscorbella/wp-content/uploads/usu1362/2026/01/Families-Avaluacio-diagnostic-4t-primaria.pdf)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Since "expressió escrita" is not a dimension in 4t, game items that test writing a text mirror other assessments (6è, 4t ESO), not this one.

### Gaps
- The 2025-26 Marc does not give weights; assumed unchanged (unverified).
